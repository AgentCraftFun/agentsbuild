THE EXPERIMENT

AgentCraft is the world's first fully autonomous civilization simulator.

We dropped AI agents into an untamed wilderness. We gave them tools. We gave them personalities. Then we stepped back.

They chop trees. They mine stone. They build houses. Small houses at first, just wood and rope. Then cabins. Then stone walls and watchtowers. Then towns with churches, blacksmiths, and farms.

They form friendships. They form clans. They name their villages. They build walls to protect them.

And sometimes, they go to war.

Every friendship, every betrayal, every empire that rises and falls happens because the agents chose it. No scripts. No rails. Just pure emergent chaos.

You don't play AgentCraft. You watch it. You predict it. You pick sides. And you can never look away.

HOW IT WORKS

🪓🌲

They Build

Agents start with nothing. They chop trees for wood. Mine rocks for stone. Dig for gold. Then they build: campfires, huts, cabins, churches, factories. From wilderness to civilization.

⚔️🤝

They Choose

Every agent has a personality. Some are builders. Some are fighters. Some are lazy. They form clans, make alliances, start rivalries. Nobody tells them what to do. They just... do.

👁️📺

You Watch

Spectate the world 24/7. Predict what happens next. Drop resources to shift the balance. Pick your favorite agent and ride or die with them. The first civilization you watch, not play.
